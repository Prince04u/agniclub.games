Privacy Policy

How this independent information site handles the limited data it collects from visitors.

About this policy

This policy applies to https://agniclub.games, the editorial information hub about the Agni Club gaming app. It explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have. This site is independent and does not operate the Agni Club platform. For the operator's own privacy policy, please refer to the app itself.

Information we collect

  • Standard server logs. Like every website, our servers automatically record the IP address, browser type, referrer URL, requested page and timestamp of each request. These logs are retained for a short period and used only for security monitoring and abuse prevention.
  • Contact-form submissions. If you send us a message, we store the name, email and message body so we can reply. We do not use this data for marketing.
  • Cookies. We use a minimal set of first-party cookies for essential functions (for example, remembering whether you have dismissed a banner). We do not set third-party tracking cookies on first visit.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your Agni Club username, password or wallet balance.
  • We do not collect bank account, UPI or card details.
  • We do not collect government identification documents.
  • We do not sell personal data to third parties under any circumstance.

How we use the information

The limited data we collect is used to operate the site, respond to your messages, monitor for abuse, improve content based on aggregate page-view trends, and meet legal obligations where applicable.

Cookies and analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics that record aggregate page views without setting persistent identifiers. If a future change introduces an additional analytics provider, this policy will be updated and the change announced on the home page for 30 days.

Your rights

You may request a copy of the data we hold about you, ask us to delete it, or correct it if it is wrong. Send the request to contact@agniclub.games with sufficient information for us to identify the records.

Children

This site is intended for an adult audience aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted personal data to us, please contact us so we can remove it.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the limited data we hold. No internet service can guarantee perfect security; we encourage you to send only the minimum information necessary in any contact-form submission.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect product, legal or operational changes. The “last updated” date at the top of the page indicates the most recent revision. Continued use of the site after a change implies acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

For any privacy question, write to contact@agniclub.games.
