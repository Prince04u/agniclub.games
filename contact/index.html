We're listening

Contact the editorial team

Corrections, questions, partnership enquiries and reader feedback are all welcome. We aim to reply within two working days.

Reach us by email

General enquiries, corrections and reader feedback: contact@agniclub.games.

For platform-specific issues

This site is an independent information hub. We cannot resolve account, deposit or withdrawal issues on the Agni Club platform. For those, please use the in-app support function inside the Agni Club app — that is the only channel with the authority to access your account.

Reporting fraud or impersonation

If you have spotted a cloned site, a fake gift-code page or someone impersonating us on social media, send the URL and a short description to the email above. We monitor abuse reports and pass material cases to the relevant operator and platform.

Editorial corrections

If a guide on this site appears outdated or inaccurate, please write to us with the page URL and the specific paragraph that needs review. Our editors recheck the platform behaviour and update the page promptly. Correction credit is given when readers want it.

Office hours

Editorial replies are sent Monday to Friday, 10:00–18:00 IST. Weekend messages are answered on the next working day.

Send us a message

Please don't include passwords, OTPs or full bank details — we never need them.
