How Colour Prediction Works on Agni Club

The full mechanic of colour prediction on Agni Club: round timers, colour-to-number mapping, payout multipliers and the most common beginner mistakes.

Colour prediction in a sentence

Colour prediction is a timed lottery-style game where the player guesses the outcome — green, red, violet, or a specific number — before the round closes, and the platform reveals the result at the end of the timer. It is fast, easy to learn, and one of the most popular game types on the Agni Club lobby.

Round timers

Rooms are organised by timer length: 30 seconds, 1 minute, 3 minutes and 5 minutes. Shorter rooms deliver fast feedback but compress decision-making; longer rooms allow more time to review the recent history and place a measured stake. Many experienced players stay on a single timer length per session to keep their rhythm.

Colour-to-number mapping

Numbers 0–9 are coloured as follows on a typical Agni Club table:

  • Green: 1, 3, 7, 9
  • Red: 2, 4, 6, 8
  • Violet: 0, 5

Because 0 and 5 are tagged violet, betting violet pays the highest multiplier — it is the rarest hit.

Payouts and multipliers

Typical published multipliers:

BetMultiplier
Green or Red~2×
Violet~4.5×
Specific number~9×

Multipliers shown in the app override any value above; check the live screen before placing a stake.

A worked round

Suppose you stake ₹100 on Green in a 1-minute room. The round closes at 60 seconds and the platform publishes a result of 7. Because 7 is green, your stake returns ~2× — about ₹200, of which ₹100 is profit. If the result had been 4 (red), your stake would have been lost. If it had been 5 (violet), the green bet would also have lost, but a violet bet would have returned ~4.5× the stake.

Common beginner mistakes

  • Chasing losses. Increasing stake size to recover a loss is the biggest mistake in the game. Walk away from a losing streak.
  • Believing in “patterns”. Round results are independent. A streak of green does not raise the probability of red on the next round.
  • Playing tired. Late-night sessions amplify financial risk. Set a session clock.
  • Ignoring the bankroll. A simple rule: do not stake more than 5% of your session budget on a single round.

Responsible-play notes

Colour prediction is fun precisely because the rounds resolve quickly. That speed is also a risk factor: small losses compound rapidly, and the dopamine cycle is intense. Treat this game as entertainment, set a budget before you open the room, and stop the moment play stops being fun. The Responsible Gaming page covers the full guidelines.

FAQs

Is colour prediction skill-based?

No. Outcomes are determined by the platform's random number generator. No external strategy can predict results.

What is the minimum bet?

On most rounds, the minimum is ₹1. Maximums vary by VIP level.

Can I bet on more than one colour?

Yes, but each is a separate stake. Combined coverage reduces upside, not risk.

How long is a round?

Typically 30 seconds, 1 minute, 3 minutes or 5 minutes depending on the room you choose.
