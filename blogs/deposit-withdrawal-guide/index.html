Deposits and Withdrawals on Agni Club: A Complete Guide

How money moves on Agni Club: UPI deposits, bank-account binding, processing windows, daily limits and how to fix a stuck withdrawal.

Money flow on Agni Club

Three actions move money on the platform: a deposit adds funds, gameplay changes the wallet balance up or down, and a withdrawal releases funds to a verified bank account or UPI ID. Each step has its own screen and its own validation rules — and understanding them prevents the most common wallet headaches.

Making a deposit

  1. Open the app and tap Wallet → Add balance.
  2. Enter the amount and choose a payment method (UPI is the most common).
  3. Confirm the payment in your UPI app.
  4. The deposit credits within seconds. If a deposit shows as pending for more than 15 minutes, contact in-app support with the transaction reference.

Submitting a withdrawal

  1. Open Wallet → Withdraw.
  2. If you have not yet bound your bank account, the app prompts you to do so. Enter holder name, account number and IFSC carefully. Mismatches between holder name and the registered mobile number are the leading cause of withdrawal rejection.
  3. Enter the amount.
  4. Review and submit.

Limits and processing windows

  • Minimum withdrawal: around ₹100 (may vary).
  • Daily withdrawal cap: set per account tier; the cap rises with VIP level.
  • Processing time: usually a few hours within business windows; longer over public holidays.
  • Settlement on the bank side: instantaneous to the beneficiary once released; check your bank's SMS log.

Fixing a stuck withdrawal

Three causes account for nearly every stuck withdrawal:

  1. Name mismatch. The bank-account holder name differs from the name on the registered mobile number. Fix in Profile and re-submit.
  2. Outdated IFSC. Branch mergers occasionally invalidate older IFSC codes. Check with your bank, update and re-submit.
  3. Daily cap reached. If today's request takes you above the cap, the request is queued until the next day.

If none of the three apply, send a screenshot (with balances blurred) and the request ID to in-app support.

Wallet safety

Treat your bank-binding screen like your banking app: do not share screenshots. Never approve a payment request you did not initiate. If anyone asks for your OTP — including a person claiming to be from “support” — refuse and end the conversation. Real platform support never needs your OTP.

FAQs

What is the minimum deposit?

Typically ₹100. Limits vary by region and may change.

What is the minimum withdrawal?

Usually around ₹100 once your bank account is bound.

How long do withdrawals take?

Most requests settle within a few hours during business windows.

Can I withdraw to a wallet?

Withdrawals are released to bank account or UPI ID only. Third-party wallets are not supported as the destination.
