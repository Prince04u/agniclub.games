Inside the Agent Referral Program

A clear-eyed explainer of the Agni Club agent referral program: how commissions are calculated, what the tiers reward, and worked examples for small and large teams.

What the program is

Agni Club rewards existing players who invite new members. The program has three layers: a one-time bonus paid when an invitee makes a qualifying first deposit, a recurring daily-salary structure for agents whose teams hit certain deposit thresholds, and an ads-fee support amount for agents who self-fund promotional campaigns.

Levels and rewards

The agent referral super-bonus chart sets the headline numbers. The chart rewards both the number of invited subordinates and the minimum deposits they make.

How rewards are calculated

Two conditions must be met: the invite must register through your code, and the invite must make a deposit (and, in some cases, a minimum bet) that reaches the published threshold. The reward credits to your wallet automatically once both conditions are satisfied. Manual claims are not normally required.

Rules that often surprise new agents

  • Both conditions must hit. Invitations without deposits do not earn the bonus, even if the invitee is active otherwise.
  • Cheating cancels bonuses. Multi-accounting, fake mobile numbers or device-farms are detected and reverse the bonus.
  • One person, one account. Same IP / same bank detail / same phone number rules apply.
  • Bonuses settle on platform schedule. Salary payouts happen on the operator's daily settlement window.

Ethical promotion

The fastest way to lose long-term agent income is to over-promise on social channels. Stick to factual descriptions of what the platform offers, never guarantee wins, and respect every responsible-gaming rule. Recruiting people who later regret their decision is bad for them and bad for your sustainable earnings.

FAQs

Who qualifies as an agent?

Any existing player with at least one successful invite that meets the platform's minimum activity threshold.

Are there any caps on rewards?

There is no cap on referral count; per-deposit bonus amounts follow the published tier.

How are fake referrals detected?

Standard checks include device fingerprint, IP address, payment method and behavioural patterns. Detected fake referrals forfeit all related rewards.

Is the income guaranteed?

No. Earnings depend on the activity of your invites. Real-money gaming is volatile.
