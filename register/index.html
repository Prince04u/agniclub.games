Introduction to Agni Club Register

Registration is the single most important step for a new Agni Club player. The decisions you make in the first 90 seconds — which mobile number to use, which password to set, whether to opt in to marketing messages — shape every future interaction with the platform. This guide treats registration as a small project: we cover what you need before you start, what to enter at each prompt, and what to do immediately after so the account is ready for safe, productive play.

Agni Club is distributed as an Android APK. There is no desktop or iOS client at the time of writing, so the registration flow described here is the only official path to a new account. Always start the registration from the app downloaded from agniclub.games — repackaged APKs from third-party sites are the most common source of compromised accounts.

Eligibility & prerequisites

You must be at least 18 years old to register. Beyond age, you need three things in hand: a working mobile number that can receive SMS for OTP delivery, a device that meets the minimum Android version specified in the install screen, and a quiet two-minute window so you do not interrupt the OTP step. Players who attempt registration over an unstable connection occasionally end up in an in-between state where the OTP screen disappears before the code arrives — restarting on a stable network resolves this every time.

The five-step registration walkthrough

  1. Open the app and tap Register. The button sits beside the Login button on the welcome screen.
  2. Enter your mobile number. Double-check the digits — this is the identifier the platform uses for every future security action, including password reset.
  3. Verify the OTP. A six-digit one-time password arrives via SMS. Type it in or use the auto-fill prompt if your device supports it.
  4. Set a password. Use at least eight characters, mix letters and numbers, and avoid passwords reused on other apps. The password field accepts symbols if you prefer to add them.
  5. Confirm and finish. Optionally enter an invitation code, accept the terms, and tap Submit. You land on the lobby with a zero-balance wallet ready to receive your first deposit.

Account verification and KYC

Initial registration verifies your mobile number only. A second tier of verification is required before withdrawals are released — typically bank-account binding (account number, IFSC code, holder name) or a UPI ID. This second step protects you: it ensures withdrawals can only be sent to the account in your own name, blocking the most common form of social-engineering theft.

Using an invitation code

If a friend referred you, enter their invitation code in the optional field during registration. The code does not change anything in your account configuration; it simply tags your account so a small referral reward is credited to the inviter once you reach the platform's minimum activity threshold (usually a first deposit and a minimum total bet amount).

Common mistakes new players make

  • Typing the wrong mobile number. A typo here means the OTP never arrives and the password-reset flow becomes useless.
  • Reusing a banking password. Never share a password with your bank login — one breach should not compromise both.
  • Skipping bank binding. Withdrawal cannot proceed without it. Bind the account well before you need it.
  • Trusting forwarded APKs. Download only from the official site.

Registration FAQs

How long does registration take?

On a stable connection the full registration completes in about 90 seconds, including OTP verification.

Do I need an invitation code?

No. Invitation codes are optional. They add a small starter bonus for the inviter once you meet the platform's minimum activity threshold.

Is there an age requirement?

Yes. Agni Club is strictly for users aged 18 years and above. Underage registration is prohibited.

Can I register without a mobile number?

No. A valid mobile number is the only supported account identifier and is required for OTP verification and password recovery.

Can one person register multiple accounts?

No. The platform allows one account per person and per device. Multiple accounts are typically detected and may be suspended.

Will I receive marketing SMS?

Transactional messages (OTP, withdrawal confirmation) are always sent. Promotional SMS opt-in is shown during registration and can be changed later in settings.
