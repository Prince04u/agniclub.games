Introduction to Agni Club Login

The Agni Club login screen is the gateway to every other action on the platform — from placing a colour-prediction bet to withdrawing your winnings. It looks simple by design, but a small amount of preparation turns a routine sign-in into a reliable two-second action. This guide walks you through the official process, then explains what to do when something goes wrong, and finishes with the security habits that experienced players use to protect their accounts.

Because Agni Club is distributed as an Android app rather than a desktop browser product, the login experience is identical on every Android handset that meets the minimum specification. The only variations come from carrier-level OTP delays and the occasional permission prompt the operating system shows on first launch.

What you need before logging in

Before you tap the green Login button, confirm three things: the latest APK is installed, the SIM card linked to your account is active in the device receiving the OTP, and the device has stable mobile data or Wi-Fi. Login failures are almost always caused by one of these three items being out of order, not by a problem on the platform.

  • Latest app version. Older builds occasionally lose compatibility with new security checks. Update from agniclub.games if the version number in Settings is below 1.4.
  • Registered mobile number. The account is tied to the number you used at registration. A new SIM with a ported number works only after the carrier confirms the port-in.
  • Password. If you use a password manager, allow autofill access. If you set the password months ago and have not used it since, run a forgot-password reset rather than risk a lockout.

Step-by-step login process

The full process takes less than 30 seconds on a clean install. Follow the steps below precisely the first time; after that, the app remembers your number and the flow becomes a single tap.

  1. Open the Agni Club app. The splash screen loads in under two seconds. If it hangs, force-close and reopen.
  2. Tap the Login button at the top of the welcome screen. New users tap Register instead.
  3. Enter your registered mobile number. Include the country code if prompted; do not paste numbers that include hyphens or spaces.
  4. Enter your password. If the field shows a “show” icon, briefly toggle it on to verify there are no typos before submitting.
  5. Complete the captcha if the app challenges you. This is a basic bot-protection layer and refreshes if you tap the image.
  6. Tap Submit. On success, you land on the lobby with your wallet balance visible at the top.

Common login issues and fixes

Three errors account for the majority of support requests in any mobile gaming app. Each has a quick fix that resolves the issue without contacting support.

1. OTP never arrives

If the SMS does not reach you within 60 seconds, check your carrier signal, confirm SMS is not silently blocked by a security app, and request a fresh code. If the second attempt also fails, switch to a Wi-Fi connection so the device can fall back to an in-app push notification where available.

2. “Incorrect password” loop

This message appears after even a single wrong attempt. Rather than retry blindly, use Forgot Password to reset cleanly. Repeated wrong attempts trigger a temporary 15-minute lockout designed to slow brute-force attacks.

3. Account locked

If the app shows an account-locked message, wait the displayed cooldown period (usually 15–30 minutes) before trying again. If the lock persists, contact in-app support with your registered mobile number — never share screenshots that show your password field.

Login security best practices

  • Use a unique password you do not reuse anywhere else.
  • Never share your OTP, even with someone claiming to be from support.
  • Disable “last login auto-fill” on shared devices.
  • Enable biometric unlock if your device supports it — it speeds up daily login and protects against shoulder-surfing.
  • Review the Account Security screen every few weeks to confirm no unfamiliar device is listed.

Forgot password — recovery flow

The recovery flow uses your registered mobile number as the only authoritative identifier. The sequence is: tap Forgot Password → enter mobile → request OTP → confirm OTP → choose new password. New passwords should be at least eight characters long and mix letters with numbers. Symbols are accepted but not required by the current app build.

Alternate login methods

For accounts that have completed first-time password login, the app supports fingerprint and face-unlock on supported devices. These options live under Settings → Account Security. There is no third-party social login (Google, Facebook, Apple) — login is intentionally limited to mobile + password to keep the account graph simple and reduce phishing attack surface.

Login FAQs

Can I log in to Agni Club from a browser?

The platform is delivered primarily as an Android APK, so login flows through the installed app rather than a web client. If a desktop browser session is required, support typically directs users to the mobile app instead.

What happens if I forget my password?

Tap the Forgot Password link on the login screen, verify with the OTP sent to your registered mobile number, and create a new password. The reset works only with the original number on file.

Why is the OTP not arriving?

OTP delivery depends on your carrier. Check signal strength, confirm SMS isn't blocked, and request a fresh OTP after 60 seconds. Network outages on the carrier side are the most common cause.

Is biometric login supported?

Many recent app versions allow fingerprint unlock once a successful password login is registered. Enable it under Account Security after the first login.

Can two devices stay logged in?

Most mobile gaming apps limit active sessions to one device at a time to protect the wallet. Logging in on a second device will sign out the first.

Does login require KYC documents?

Basic login uses mobile + password only. KYC details (bank account or UPI) are requested separately, at the point of withdrawal.
