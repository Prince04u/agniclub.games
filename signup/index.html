Agni Club Signup — what to expect, end to end

A plain-language guide for first-time players who want to know exactly what happens when they create an account, what rewards exist on day one and which security toggles to switch on before placing any wager.

What signup means

Signup is the moment your phone number, password and the platform begin a relationship. On Agni Club, signup is the same operation as registration — you choose your mobile number as the account identifier, set a password and confirm with a one-time code delivered by SMS. Once you tap Submit, your account exists permanently in the operator's database, and every future action — depositing, winning, withdrawing — is tied to it.

This page exists because the words “signup” and “register” are used interchangeably across the industry, and newcomers often hunt for a different process under each name. There is no second flow to learn: if you completed registration, you have signed up.

Signup vs registration

Some platforms distinguish between “signup” (open the account) and “registration” (provide KYC details). Agni Club does not. The first OTP-verified mobile number is the account, and KYC fields like bank-account number are requested separately, at the point of withdrawal. The benefit of this approach is speed: you can browse the lobby and read promotions within two minutes of installing the app.

Five-step signup flow

  1. Open the app and tap Register (the “signup” button).
  2. Enter your mobile number and request the OTP.
  3. Confirm the six-digit OTP that arrives via SMS.
  4. Set a strong, unique password.
  5. Accept the terms and finalise the account.

Post-signup checklist

  • Open Account → Profile and confirm your mobile number is correct.
  • Open Account → Security and enable biometric unlock if supported by your device.
  • Open Wallet → Bank and bind your bank account or UPI ID before you place any meaningful wager.
  • Read the in-app responsible-gaming notice; we recommend you set a personal session budget right now.

Account safety setup

The two biggest threats to a new account are credential reuse and social engineering. Avoid passwords used on other apps. Never share an OTP, even with someone who says they are from “support”. Real platform support agents do not need your OTP, your password or remote access to your phone. If a stranger asks you to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer or any screen-sharing app, end the conversation — that is the universal pattern of a phone-based gaming scam.

Signup FAQs

Is signup the same as registration?

Yes — on Agni Club the two terms are used interchangeably. Both refer to creating a new account with a verified mobile number.

Do I get a bonus simply for signing up?

Pure signup bonuses are uncommon; rewards usually unlock once you complete bank binding and your first deposit.

Can I sign up using only Wi-Fi?

Yes, as long as the SIM in the device is active and can receive the OTP SMS. Mobile data is not required if Wi-Fi is stable.

How do I delete my account later?

Account deletion is handled through in-app support. The platform may apply a cooldown to ensure no pending withdrawals are in transit.
